What sets it apart

The data most CMAs leave out.

Every CMA has comparable sales. Homesolo adds the evidence that explains a number and lets you defend it.

  • The assessor’s own condition grade

    What the city recorded about the home’s condition, not only the listing description.

  • Dated building permits

    Roofs, heating and cooling, kitchens and more, with the year the work was permitted.

  • The home’s own last sale

    Blended in, moved by its market, when it is two to six years old.

  • What didn’t sell

    Nearby listings that were withdrawn or expired, and this home’s own listing history.

  • Sales the MLS never saw

    Off-market and for-sale-by-owner transfers from the District’s public records.

  • Supply at this price point

    Months of supply for the price band the home sits in.

  • What rates did after the comps sold

    How far mortgage rates have moved, and what that means for a buyer’s budget.

  • A rent check and a cost check

    Two cross-checks shown beside the comparable sales.

Permits, assessor records, off-market sales and the cost check come from District of Columbia public records and apply to DC properties. The rest applies across DC, Maryland and Virginia.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Homesolo for?

Licensed real estate brokers, agents and salespeople working in DC, Maryland, and Virginia. We verify every license by hand before the workbench unlocks.

What is a CMA, and how does Homesolo build one?

A comparative market analysis estimates what a home is worth from comparable recent sales. Homesolo selects the comparable sales, adjusts them for differences such as size and condition, and shows the value range together with the evidence behind it. The pricing math is deterministic, so the same inputs give the same numbers.

Where does the market data come from?

Homesolo works from the Bright MLS feed under a data license. Listing data is shown with its required broker attribution and a freshness stamp.

How much does Homesolo cost?

The founding beta is free, with full platform access and free training from the founders. We will share pricing well before the beta ends.

When does the free beta start?

When your license has been verified and your workbench is unlocked, not when you sign up, so the time you have is time you can actually use the product.

How does broker approval work?

After you sign up we check your license and MLS membership by hand. Until that is done the workbench stays locked. It exists to make sure the tools go only to licensed brokers.

Can Homesolo tell me when to change a listing’s price?

It reviews your live listings against the market every week and tracks buyer response at check-ins: showings, second showings, offers, and what buyers said. It then lays out what to consider and why. It is guidance for you as the broker, not automatic and not a forecast, and nothing changes a price or contacts anyone on its own.

What is Joy, and what can she see?

Joy is Homesolo’s assistant for licensed brokers. She knows the CMA, offer or transaction you are looking at, explains Homesolo’s analysis, reads an offer, and drafts messages to your clients. She works from Homesolo’s own figures and placeholders: client names, emails, phone numbers and property addresses you have saved in Homesolo are swapped for placeholders before anything is sent, and filled back in on your screen. Text you type is sent as written, so say “my seller” rather than a name. She gives decision support, not legal advice. You review and send everything yourself.

How does Homesolo handle fair housing?

Every generated description is checked against a fair-housing prohibited-terms list before it is delivered. Violations are blocked and logged.

How accurate is the AI?

AI output is informational and is not a professional appraisal or legal advice. Pricing math is deterministic; AI narration explains it. You are responsible for reviewing all output.

How is my data handled, and is any AI trained on it?

Your data is scoped to your account with row-level security. MLS listing data is never sent to the AI; only abstracted summaries are. No model is trained on MLS data, and our AI provider’s commercial terms do not train on what we send it.
