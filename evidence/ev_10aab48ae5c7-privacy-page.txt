      - heading "Public property records" [level=2]
      - paragraph: "Assessor records, building permits and recorded sales for the District of Columbia, Maryland and Northern Virginia come from those governments’ own public files: the DC Office of Tax and Revenue and the DC Department of Buildings (published by DC GIS); Maryland’s Department of Assessments and Taxation, including its assessment (CAMA) building file and its file of recorded sales (published by Maryland iMAP); the building-permit files of Montgomery County and Prince George’s County; and the real-estate assessment and sales files of Fairfax County (Department of Tax Administration), Arlington County (Department of Real Estate Assessments) and Loudoun County.       - heading "MLS data" [level=2]
      - paragraph: Property listing data from Bright MLS is used solely to display real estate information within our platform. MLS data is not transmitted to any AI provider. Bright MLS data is not used to train or improve any AI model.
      - heading "AI processing" [level=2]
      - paragraph: We use Anthropic’s API for inference only. Your content — the information you provide and the AI-generated outputs you receive — is not used to train any AI model, by Anthropic or anyone else. MLS listing data is never sent to any AI provider; the AI receives only abstracted summaries.
      - paragraph: "Joy, the in-app assistant, works from Homesolo’s own figures and placeholders. Client names, email addresses, phone numbers and property addresses that you have saved in Homesolo are replaced with placeholders before anything is sent, and we put the real details back into her answer after it returns. Text you type yourself is sent as you wrote it, so a name that is not saved in Homesolo, or a spelling that differs from the saved one, may not be replaced: say “my seller” instead of a name. The same is true of other AI features that take free text or a document you upload (listing descriptions, counter-offers, questions about an analysis, contract reading), which send what you give them. We keep a record that a question was asked and what it cost, not its text."
