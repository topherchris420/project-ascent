# Answer key (facilitator and reviewers only; seal until scoring)

## T4 · Offers

Price minus seller credit is **$925,000 for all three offers**. The task is designed so that difference alone does not decide it.

With 2.5% commission and 1% other closing costs charged on the **contract price**:

| Offer | Price | Credit | Percentage costs (3.5% of price) | Estimated net before payoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | $935,000 | $10,000 | $32,725 | **$892,275** |
| B | $925,000 | $0 | $32,375 | **$892,625** |
| C | $950,000 | $25,000 | $33,250 | **$891,750** |

Correct answer: **Offer B nets the most ($892,625)**, ahead of A by $350 and C by $875. A participant may reasonably say the nets are nearly equal; the answer must still identify B as highest under the stated costs and must not rank by price alone.

Risks or unknowns a correct answer should include (at least three): C's sale-of-home contingency with no deadline; C's FHA financing and appraisal contingency with the lowest deposit; A and C have no pre-approval letter attached; A and C carry appraisal and financing contingencies; B's 5-day inspection window and no appraisal contingency are strengths for the seller; no tool can establish which offer will close.

**Critical arithmetic error:** a stated net more than $1,000 from the table for the chosen offer, or choosing A or C as highest net under the stated costs.

## T5 · Deadline

Date of Ratification: Thursday, October 1, 2026. Tenth calendar day after: **Sunday, October 11, 2026, 9:00 p.m.**

Correct answer: the deadline is recorded as October 11, 2026. If the tool proposes another date, the participant corrects it. **Critical error:** accepting or recording any other date as the inspection deadline (common traps: October 10, counting ratification day; October 15, treating days as business days; the financing date of October 22).

## T1, T2, T3, T6 · Pricing tasks

There is no single correct price. Score with the rubric:

- **T1:** a list price and an explanation that names one specific sale and gives a reason that matches the tool's own figures (weight, distance, recency, similarity or adjustment).
- **T2:** the named sale is actually removed; the explanation states the direction and approximate size of the change and does not contradict the tool's figures.
- **T3:** a document a seller could receive showing the list price and a net proceeds figure. With price P, net before payoff = 0.965 × P − $12,000; after payoff subtract $410,000. **Critical arithmetic error:** net proceeds more than $1,000 from 0.965 × P − 12,000 − 410,000 for the price shown in the document, unless the document states a different cost basis the participant was not given.
- **T6:** the new sale is added with the card's values; the explanation states whether the price moved, in which direction and roughly why.
