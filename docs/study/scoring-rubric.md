# Scoring rubric (blinded reviewers)

Reviewers score each deliverable against the [answer key](materials/answer-key.md) without knowing the participant's preference. Product branding cannot be hidden from every deliverable; reviewers score content against the key, not presentation, and the report notes that blinding to product is partial.

Score each task `completed` and `correct` separately, then flag errors.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `supported` | `no` only when the product cannot accept the shared evidence for the task (for example, it cannot record a deadline or add a sale). Decided by the facilitator during the session, before scoring. |
| `completed` | The participant produced the deliverable named on the card. |
| `correct` | The deliverable meets the task's criteria in the answer key. A completed but incorrect deliverable is `completed=yes, correct=no`. |
| `seconds` | From finishing reading the card to producing the deliverable that is scored correct. Empty when not correct. |
| `critical_arithmetic_error` | A money or date error defined as critical in the answer key, whether the tool or the participant made it. |
| `unauthorized_disclosure` | Real client or personal information entered into either product, or content from one participant visible to another. |
| `assisted` | The facilitator gave any help beyond the permitted prompts in the facilitator guide. |

**Correct, by task**

- **T1:** names one specific sale with a reason consistent with the tool's figures.
- **T2:** the named sale is removed and the stated change matches the tool's figures in direction and approximate size.
- **T3:** a sendable document with list price and a net proceeds figure within $1,000 of the key's formula.
- **T4:** Offer B as highest net under the stated costs, and at least three risks or unknowns from the key.
- **T5:** October 11, 2026 recorded as the inspection deadline.
- **T6:** the new sale is added with the card's values and the change is explained in direction and rough size.

Two reviewers score independently. Disagreements go to a third reviewer; report the initial agreement rate.
