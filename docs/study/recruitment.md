# Recruitment, screening and consent

## Eligibility

- Active real estate license in DC, Maryland or Virginia.
- At least two years of practice, with a listing or buyer transaction in the District in the past 24 months.
- Uses a desktop or laptop browser at work.
- Not employed by, contracted to, or a close relative or friend of anyone on the Ascent or HomeSolo teams.

Aim for at least four primarily-listing and four primarily-buyer agents among the ten. Record prior use of either product; do not exclude for it.

## Screener (record answers against a pseudonymous ID)

1. Which states are you licensed in, and since when?
2. In the past 24 months, roughly how many listings and buyer transactions did you handle in Washington, DC?
3. Is your work mostly listings, mostly buyers, or about even?
4. Have you used Ascent or HomeSolo before? If so, how much?
5. Do you have any personal or business relationship with people at either company?

## Outreach (neutral wording)

> We're running a two-hour research session comparing two real estate pricing and transaction tools. You'll complete six short professional tasks in each and tell us which you'd prefer to use. It's paid at [rate], set in advance and the same whatever you conclude. The study is funded by the developer of one of the tools; an independent facilitator runs it. Interested? Reply and we'll send a five-question screener.

Recruit through channels neither team controls (for example, a local association bulletin or a research panel), not through either product's customer lists or social accounts.

## Consent (participant signs before the session)

- **Purpose:** compare two real estate tools on six tasks and record which you prefer.
- **Sponsor:** the developer of Ascent funds the study. The facilitator is independent and you may say anything about either tool.
- **What we record:** task deliverables, times, your preference and reasons. Screen recording only if you tick the box below.
- **Your data:** stored under a pseudonymous ID. Your name and contact details are kept separately and deleted within 90 days. Published results never identify you.
- **No client data:** do not enter real client names, contacts or transaction details into either tool. All task materials are fictional or public records.
- **Voluntary:** you may skip any task or stop at any time and still be paid.
- **Payment:** [rate], the same regardless of your answers.

☐ I agree to take part. ☐ I agree to screen recording for scoring only (deleted after scoring).

Signature, date, facilitator signature.
