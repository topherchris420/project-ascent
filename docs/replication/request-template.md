# Request to a prospective independent reviewer

A message the maintainers can adapt and send. Ascent does not send it automatically, and nothing here should be read as a reviewer having agreed to anything.

---

**Subject:** Independent replication of a home-valuation accuracy study

Hello [name],

Ascent is a free, open-source tool that helps homeowners understand what recorded sales say about a home’s value. We publish out-of-sample accuracy studies for our valuation method, including the ones that failed, and we would like an outside quantitative researcher to reproduce them and assess the methodology. No one outside the project has done so yet.

What it involves:

- Six retrospective studies on public recorded sales (Washington, DC; King County, WA; and Maryland), each with a pre-registered protocol, per-sale results and nine fixed acceptance criteria.
- A one-command replication run (`npm run replicate`), an independent re-derivation in Python, a written method specification for reimplementing the valuations, and a ten-question methodology rubric.
- Your conclusions in a record you sign with your own key. We publish it unedited, including unfavorable findings, and change no published result in response.

You choose the depth: re-deriving every figure takes an afternoon; reimplementing the method takes several days. [Compensation: amount and terms, agreed before work starts and independent of conclusions, or “We cannot offer payment.”]

Everything is in the repository: https://github.com/topherchris420/project-ascent/blob/master/docs/independent-replication.md

Please tell us about any relationship with the project or its maintainers that could affect your independence. If you are not the right person, we would be grateful for a suggestion.

Thank you,
[maintainer]
