# Consent form · Ascent home-value comprehension study

Please read this with the facilitator and ask any question before deciding.

**What this is.** Ascent is a free, open-source tool that explains what recorded home sales suggest about a home’s value. We want to learn whether the explanations are understandable to homeowners. We are testing the website, not you.

**What you will do.** For about 60 minutes you will look at fictional practice homes on the website, answer nine questions in your own words while thinking aloud, and fill in a ten-question questionnaire. You will never be asked to enter your own mortgage, income, address or other personal finances. Afterwards you may look up an address of your choice; that part is optional and not recorded beyond the facilitator’s notes.

**What we record.** Your answers, how long each question took, whether you needed a hint, and the facilitator’s notes. [If the session is recorded: audio and screen recording, used only to check the notes and deleted within 30 days.] We use a code such as P07 instead of your name.

**What we publish.** Counts of how many participants understood each part, your exact words where they show how the website was understood or misunderstood (under your code, never your name), and accessibility barriers. Names, contact details, recordings and this form are never published.

**Your rights.** Taking part is voluntary. You may skip any question or stop at any time without giving a reason, and you will still receive [compensation]. You may ask us to remove your answers before results are published.

**Risks and benefits.** There are no known risks beyond ordinary computer use. The study may help make home-value information clearer for other homeowners. Nothing in the session is financial, legal or real estate advice.
