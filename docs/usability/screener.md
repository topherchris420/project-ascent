# Screener · `ascent-usability-brief-1`

Ask these before scheduling. Keep answers only as the participant code and the categories recorded in the results; do not keep names or contact details with the study data.

## Eligibility

1. Do you own a home, or expect to sell one within the next two years? (yes → continue; no → not eligible)
2. Is that home in Washington, DC, or in Montgomery or Prince George’s County, Maryland? (yes → continue; no → not eligible)
3. Are you a licensed real estate agent or broker, an appraiser, or a mortgage lender or loan officer? (yes → not eligible)
4. Are you 18 or older? (no → not eligible)

## Recruitment variety (not eligibility)

Record each answer to balance the group. The protocol requires at least half on a phone, at least two who use a screen reader or magnification, both house and condo owners, and more than one age band.

5. Which would you use for a 60-minute session: a phone, a tablet or a computer?
6. Do you use a screen reader, screen magnification or large text settings? (screen reader / magnification / other / none)
7. Is your home a house (including a rowhouse or townhouse) or a condominium?
8. Which age group are you in: 18–34, 35–49, 50–64, or 65 and over?

## Tell every candidate

- The session takes about 60 minutes, in person or by video, and uses fictional practice homes.
- They will never be asked for their own mortgage, income or other finances.
- The compensation, agreed in advance, is paid even if they stop early.
- Their name and any recording are never published.
